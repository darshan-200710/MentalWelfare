# Welfare escalation

Escalation means connecting a person with appropriate human support, not reporting them for discipline.

## Levels

- **Private welfare check-in:** a trained, authorized welfare officer or counselor contacts the person discreetly and explains confidentiality limits.
- **Urgent clinical support:** the person is encouraged to contact a qualified mental-health professional promptly, especially when symptoms are worsening or impairing daily functioning.
- **Emergency help:** immediate danger, a recent attempt, a stated plan and intent, severe confusion, or a medical emergency. Encourage local emergency services or the organizationΓÇÖs emergency pathway now, and encourage the person not to remain alone.
