# Burnout

Burnout-like patterns can include prolonged exhaustion, cynicism or detachment, reduced sense of effectiveness, and poor recovery in the setting of sustained demands. This description is not a clinical diagnosis.

## Support approach

Review workload, recovery, staffing, leave access, sleep, health, and autonomy with the person and authorized welfare staff. Prefer organizational remedies such as workload balancing, rest, rotation, and support access over individual blame. Escalate to clinical care when functioning or safety is affected.
