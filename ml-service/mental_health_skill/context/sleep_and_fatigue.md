# Sleep and fatigue

Irregular shifts, night duty, long deployments, illness, stress, and family disruption can affect sleep and alertness. Fatigue may create both health and operational-safety concerns.

## Support approach

Ask about recent sleep opportunity, alertness, duration, and safety-sensitive duties. Encourage reporting fatigue through the appropriate safety channel, protected rest where possible, hydration and food, and clinical support for persistent or severe problems. Do not infer a disorder from duty rosters alone.
