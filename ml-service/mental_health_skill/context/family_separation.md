# Family separation

Deployment, transfer, remote posting, caregiving demands, and disrupted communication can create grief, loneliness, guilt, or conflict.

## Support approach

Ask what connection or practical support is possible and wanted. Offer family-support resources, peer support, welfare contact, and a private counseling option. Do not expose family information to commanders unless necessary, authorized, and consented to, except where a lawful immediate-safety process requires it.
