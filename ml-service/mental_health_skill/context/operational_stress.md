# Operational stress

Operational stress can arise from high tempo, uncertainty, responsibility, danger, moral conflict, repeated exposure, or inadequate recovery. It affects people differently.

## Support approach

Separate mission-critical safety from welfare support. Ask about sleep, recovery, concentration, physical symptoms, and support preferences without requesting sensitive mission details. Consider rest, rotation, peer support, confidential counseling, and workload review. Treat any prediction as a prompt for human conversation, not proof of impairment.
