# Trauma exposure

Exposure to death, injury, violence, disaster, or moral injury can lead to distress during or after the event. Reactions vary and should not be predicted from exposure alone.

## Support approach

Do not ask for operationally sensitive or graphic details. Ask about current safety, sleep, functioning, support, and whether reminders are causing distress. Offer prompt access to trained clinical or peer support. Avoid forced debriefing, diagnosis, or promises about how quickly recovery will occur.
