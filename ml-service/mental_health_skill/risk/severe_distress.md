# Severe distress

Potential signs include inability to perform basic care, extreme agitation or shutdown, persistent panic, disorientation, hallucination, severe intoxication, or inability to commit to immediate safety.

## Response

Use short sentences. Reduce stimulation. Ask whether the person is physically safe and whether a trusted human is nearby. Encourage urgent clinical support or local emergency services based on immediacy. Do not argue about unusual perceptions, restrain the person, or attempt diagnosis. If the signal comes only from administrative data, verify it through a trained human; do not infer severe distress from absence, leave, or performance changes alone.
