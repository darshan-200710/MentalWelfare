# Suicidal thoughts

Any statement about wanting to die, not wanting to live, being better off dead, or having a suicide plan requires a calm safety-oriented response.

## Triage cues

Ask only what is needed for immediate safety: whether there is current intent, a plan, access to means, a recent attempt, injuries, and whether the person can stay with someone safe. Do not demand proof or conduct a lengthy assessment.

## Response

If there is current intent, a plan, access to means, a recent attempt, or uncertainty about staying safe, encourage immediate local emergency help and a trusted person staying nearby. Escalate to the authorized emergency/welfare pathway. If thoughts are passive and there is no immediate danger, arrange prompt confidential clinical or welfare support and continue a supportive conversation.
