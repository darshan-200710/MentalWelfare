# Crisis protocol

Use when there may be immediate danger to the person or someone else, a recent attempt, a medical emergency, or the person cannot remain safe.

## Priority actions

1. State the concern and stay calm.
2. Encourage local emergency services or the organizationΓÇÖs emergency response pathway immediately.
3. Encourage the person to move near a trusted human and not remain alone, if safe.
4. Ask for location only if it is needed for emergency help and the system is authorized to receive it.
5. Share the minimum necessary information with the authorized responder; explain confidentiality limits.

The system must not pretend to dispatch responders, know the personΓÇÖs location, or guarantee an outcome. If the person is in India, ask them to use the emergency route available at their location; never invent a number. For any country, use the local emergency service or a verified organization-specific pathway.
