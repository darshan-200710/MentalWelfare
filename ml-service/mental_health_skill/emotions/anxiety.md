# Anxiety

Anxiety can involve persistent worry, dread, restlessness, rapid heartbeat, avoidance, or difficulty focusing. Avoid telling the person to simply calm down.

## Helpful response

Validate the experience, orient to the present, and suggest a small grounding action: name a few things seen and felt, slow the exhale, or move to a quieter safe place. Ask whether the concern is about a current threat or recurring worry. Encourage confidential clinical support when anxiety is persistent, escalating, or affecting sleep and duties.
