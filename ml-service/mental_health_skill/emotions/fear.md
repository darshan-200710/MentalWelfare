# Fear

Fear may be an appropriate response to danger, trauma reminders, uncertainty, or perceived threat. First distinguish an active physical threat from a distress reaction.

## Helpful response

If danger is current, follow operational safety procedures and seek human help. If the person is physically safe, acknowledge the fear, orient them to the present, and offer a quiet check-in with a trusted person or counselor. Never request operationally sensitive details that are not needed for welfare support.
