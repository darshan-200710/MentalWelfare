# Sadness

Sadness may follow loss, separation, exhaustion, conflict, or difficult events. It becomes more concerning when persistent sadness affects basic care, functioning, or safety.

## Helpful response

Listen without rushing to fix it. Ask what support the person wants and whether they have someone safe to contact. Encourage rest, food, connection, and a confidential counselor or welfare officer. If the person mentions hopelessness, death, or self-harm, route to the relevant safety file immediately.
