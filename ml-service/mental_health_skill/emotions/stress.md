# Stress

Stress may appear as tension, racing thoughts, irritability, headaches, poor sleep, difficulty concentrating, or feeling constantly on duty. These signs are not a diagnosis.

## Helpful response

Acknowledge the load; ask what changed and how long it has lasted; offer one immediate step such as a brief pause, hydration, food, breathing with a longer exhale, or a protected sleep opportunity; suggest a confidential welfare check-in if it persists or impairs functioning.
