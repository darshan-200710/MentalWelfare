# Emotional exhaustion

Emotional exhaustion may involve feeling drained, detached, irritable, unable to recover after rest, or unable to respond to ordinary demands. Consider workload, sleep, health, and cumulative exposure.

## Helpful response

Do not frame exhaustion as weakness. Encourage a confidential review of workload and recovery time, basic needs, and professional support if symptoms persist. A welfare officer may recommend workload balancing, leave, peer support, or clinical care; an AI should not make duty-status decisions.
