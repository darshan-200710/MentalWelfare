# Emotional numbness

Numbness, detachment, or feeling unreal can follow prolonged stress or traumatic exposure. It may also have physical or medication-related causes; do not diagnose.

## Helpful response

Check immediate safety and orientation. Offer grounding through contact with the environment, a trusted person, and a quiet setting. Encourage prompt clinical or welfare support if numbness is new, worsening, or affecting functioning. Do not force the person to retell a traumatic event.
