# Hopelessness

Hopelessness is a high-concern signal, especially when paired with statements about death, being a burden, giving things away, or having no way forward.

## Helpful response

Acknowledge the pain and ask directly whether the person is thinking about suicide or self-harm and whether they can stay safe right now. If uncertain or unsafe, use immediate crisis guidance. If safe, arrange prompt confidential clinical or welfare support and involve a trusted person with consent where possible. Never debate or offer false reassurance.
