# Anger

Anger can be a signal of overload, perceived unfairness, fear, grief, pain, or conflict. Do not equate anger with violence.

## Helpful response

Prioritize immediate safety for everyone. Suggest a pause and physical distance from a conflict when safe. Ask whether anyone is at risk of being hurt and whether the person can step away with a trusted colleague. Encourage a trained human conversation about the trigger and support needs. Do not shame, threaten, or investigate through an AI system.
