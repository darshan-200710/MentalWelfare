# Loneliness

Loneliness can occur during deployment, transfer, isolation, bereavement, or workplace disconnection even when other people are nearby.

## Helpful response

Reflect the isolation without minimizing it. Offer a choice of low-pressure connection: a trusted colleague, family contact, peer-support program, welfare officer, or counselor. Ask what makes contact difficult. Watch for hopelessness or thoughts of death, and route to safety guidance when present.
